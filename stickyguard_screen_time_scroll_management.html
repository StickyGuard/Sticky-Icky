Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does StickyGuard require Accessibility Services?

Android requires Accessibility permissions for app interceptors to detect when user-selected target applications (like social media apps) enter the foreground so it can display the scripture overlay screen.

Does StickyGuard read my private messages or passwords?

No. StickyGuard does not read window content, user text inputs outside the app, messages, passwords, or personal data. It only inspects the foreground application package name.

Can I use my own Bible translations?

Yes! StickyGuard includes standard translations (NIV, ESV, KJV, NLT, CSB) and allows you to manually input custom scriptures or memory verses.

Privacy Policy & Accessibility Service Disclosure

Google Play Compliant • Updated 2026

Prominent Accessibility API Disclosure (Google Play Requirement)

StickyGuard requires the Android AccessibilityService API to perform its core screen-time interceptor functionality.

  • What is accessed: Foreground application package names (e.g., com.instagram.android).
  • Purpose: To detect when a user-designated guarded app is launched and present the scripture lockout overlay screen.
  • Data Collection & Sharing: StickyGuard does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, typed text, screen content, or browsing activity.

1. Overview

StickyGuard ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how our application interacts with your device and explains our strict non-collection data policy.

2. Use of Android AccessibilityService API

In strict compliance with Google Play Developer Policies:

  • StickyGuard uses the AccessibilityService API solely to identify when user-selected applications enter the foreground.
  • StickyGuard does not alter user settings without permission.
  • StickyGuard does not prevent users from disabling or uninstalling the app.
  • StickyGuard does not record audio, capture keystrokes, read chat messages, access contact lists, or monitor internet traffic.

3. Information Collection & Storage

All user preferences (such as selected guarded apps, custom Bible verses, and timer configurations) are stored locally on your device using Android's encrypted EncryptedSharedPreferences or Jetpack DataStore. No data ever leaves your hardware.

4. Third-Party Data Sharing

StickyGuard does not incorporate third-party tracking SDKs, analytics frameworks that read app events, or advertising networks. We do not sell, trade, or share any information with third parties.

5. Contact Information

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or Google Play compliance, contact us at privacy@stickyguard.app.
